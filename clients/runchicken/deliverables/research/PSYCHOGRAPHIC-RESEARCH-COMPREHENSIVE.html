Decision-Making Values Hierarchy

When Choosing Travel Experiences:

  1. 1. Authenticity Factor: Is this a genuine local experience or commercialized tourism? (Most Important)
  2. 2. Cultural Depth: Will I gain real understanding of the region's history, people, traditions?
  3. 3. Group Size/Intimacy: Small enough for meaningful connections and personalized experience?
  4. 4. Sustainability: Does this support local economy and minimize environmental impact?
  5. 5. Physical Challenge: Appropriate difficulty level for growth without being inaccessible?
  6. 6. Value for Investment: Price relative to experience depth and quality (Least Important)

Note: Price sensitivity is low—Cultural Explorers will pay premium for authentic experiences that align with values. They compare value quality, not price points.

Communication Do's and Don'ts for Cultural Explorers

✓ DO THESE

  • ✓ Lead with authentic stories from real participants (names, photos, specific details)
  • ✓ Emphasize small group sizes (8-12) and intimate, personal experience
  • ✓ Share guide backgrounds, personal stories, local expertise credentials
  • ✓ Highlight cultural/historical depth of routes (not just scenic beauty)
  • ✓ Use conversational, personal tone (like talking to a friend at running club)
  • ✓ Show behind-the-scenes, unpolished authentic moments

✗ AVOID THESE

  • ✗ Corporate marketing speak ("premium," "world-class," "luxury")
  • ✗ Stock photos or overly-polished professional photography
  • ✗ Hard-sell promotional messaging (focus on value/community, not "book now")
  • ✗ Generic destination descriptions (they've already Googled the basics)
  • ✗ Comparison to competitors or negative competitive framing
  • ✗ Frequent promotional emails (feels spammy, breaks trust)
