Strategic Positioning Statement

RUNCHICKEN targets experience-driven runners aged 30-55 who view running as a gateway to cultural discovery and authentic travel experiences. These customers reject mass-market running tours in favor of intimate, locally-guided adventures that combine athletic challenge with deep regional immersion. They prioritize authentic connections over Instagram moments, hidden trails over famous landmarks, and community bonding over solo achievement.

The ideal RUNCHICKEN customer values sustainable tourism practices, seeks personal growth through physical challenge, and invests in memorable experiences rather than material possessions. They are digitally connected but experience-focused, using technology to discover and book adventures while prioritizing real-world human connections during the journey.
