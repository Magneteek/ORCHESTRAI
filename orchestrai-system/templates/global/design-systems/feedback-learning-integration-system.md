### **METHOD 2: Structured Feedback Form**

**Design Elements Feedback:**
- **Color Scheme**: Rate 1-10, specific color preferences
- **Typography**: Font sizes, weights, hierarchy preferences  
- **Layout**: Spacing, organization, visual hierarchy
- **Interactive Elements**: Button styles, hover effects, animations
- **Mobile Experience**: Navigation, touch targets, responsive behavior

**Business Impact Feedback:**
- **Target Audience Alignment**: How well design matches audience
- **Conversion Elements**: CTA effectiveness, lead generation
- **Brand Representation**: Professional appearance, trust factors
- **Competitive Positioning**: Comparison to competitors

**Metrics to Track:**
- **Engagement**: Time on page, scroll depth, click-through rates
- **Conversion**: Lead generation, demo requests, contact submissions
- **Performance**: Page load speed, mobile usability scores
- **SEO Impact**: Search rankings, organic traffic changes
