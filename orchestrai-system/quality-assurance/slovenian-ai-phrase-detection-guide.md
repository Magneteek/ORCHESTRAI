# Slovenian AI Phrase Detection Guide
## Identifying Unnatural AI-Generated Language Patterns in Slovenian

### OVERVIEW

This guide identifies common patterns, phrases, and linguistic structures that betray AI-generated content in Slovenian. These patterns sound unnatural to native speakers and should be avoided for authentic communication.

---

## 1. OVERLY FORMAL ACADEMIC LANGUAGE

### ❌ **UNNATURAL AI PHRASES**

**Excessive Use of Academic Conjunctions:**
- ❌ "Posledično" (Consequently) - overused in every paragraph
- ❌ "Nadalje" (Furthermore) - repetitive academic connector
- ❌ "V skladu s tem" (In accordance with this) - overly bureaucratic
- ❌ "V kontekstu tega" (In the context of this) - academic stuffiness
- ❌ "S tem namenom" (For this purpose) - formal redundancy

**Overly Complex Sentence Starters:**
- ❌ "V okviru obravnave tematike" (Within the framework of addressing the topic)
- ❌ "Z namenom zagotavljanja" (For the purpose of ensuring)
- ❌ "V luči dejstva, da" (In light of the fact that)
- ❌ "Glede na okoliščine" (Given the circumstances)
- ❌ "Na podlagi zgoraj navedenega" (Based on the above mentioned)

### ✅ **NATURAL SLOVENIAN ALTERNATIVES**

**Simple Connectors:**
- ✅ "Zato" (Therefore)
- ✅ "Tudi" (Also)
- ✅ "Potem" (Then)
- ✅ "Še več" (What's more)
- ✅ "Seveda" (Of course)

**Natural Sentence Flow:**
- ✅ "Ko govorimo o" (When we talk about)
- ✅ "Pomembno je" (It's important)
- ✅ "Poglejmo" (Let's look at)
- ✅ "Na primer" (For example)
- ✅ "Enostavno povedano" (Simply put)

## 2. REPETITIVE PHRASE PATTERNS

### ❌ **AI REPETITIVE STRUCTURES**

**Overused Emphasis Patterns:**
- ❌ "Ključno je poudariti" (It is crucial to emphasize) - appears multiple times
- ❌ "Pomembno je omeniti" (It is important to mention) - repetitive starter
- ❌ "Vredno je opozoriti" (It is worth noting) - artificial emphasis
- ❌ "Ne smemo pozabiti" (We must not forget) - overused reminder
- ❌ "Posebej poudarjamo" (We especially emphasize) - excessive highlighting

**Redundant Conclusion Phrases:**
- ❌ "Na koncu velja poudariti" (Finally, it should be emphasized)
- ❌ "V zaključku lahko rečemo" (In conclusion, we can say)
- ❌ "Sklepno lahko ugotovimo" (In conclusion, we can determine)
- ❌ "Na osnovi vsega navedenega" (Based on everything stated)

### ✅ **NATURAL ALTERNATIVES**

**Varied Emphasis:**
- ✅ "Bistvo je v tem, da" (The point is that)
- ✅ "Glavno vprašanje je" (The main question is)
- ✅ "Posebej zanimivo je" (Particularly interesting is)
- ✅ "Tu gre za to, da" (Here it's about the fact that)

**Natural Conclusions:**
- ✅ "Skratka" (In short)
- ✅ "Torej" (So)
- ✅ "Na koncu" (In the end)
- ✅ "Pravzaprav" (Actually)

## 3. AWKWARD WORD ORDER & SYNTAX

### ❌ **UNNATURAL SENTENCE STRUCTURES**

**Passive Voice Overuse:**
- ❌ "Rezultat je bil dosežen" (The result was achieved)
- ❌ "Napredek je bil narejen" (Progress was made)
- ❌ "Odločitev je bila sprejeta" (A decision was made)
- ❌ "Analiza je bila opravljena" (Analysis was conducted)

**Awkward Word Placement:**
- ❌ "Prav gotovo je to dejstvo, ki" (Certainly, this is a fact that)
- ❌ "V veliki meri je to odvisno od" (To a large extent, this depends on)
- ❌ "V določeni meri je mogoče reči" (To a certain extent, it is possible to say)

### ✅ **NATURAL ACTIVE VOICE**

**Active Constructions:**
- ✅ "Dosegli smo rezultat" (We achieved the result)
- ✅ "Naredili smo napredek" (We made progress)
- ✅ "Sprejeli smo odločitev" (We made a decision)
- ✅ "Opravili smo analizo" (We conducted analysis)

**Natural Word Flow:**
- ✅ "To je res" (This is true)
- ✅ "To je odvisno od" (This depends on)
- ✅ "Lahko rečemo" (We can say)

- ❌ "Dandanes je znano, da" (Nowadays it is known that)

- ❌ "Postopek je popolnoma varen" (The procedure is completely safe) - unrealistic guarantee

- ❌ "Sledi obdobje okrevanja" (A recovery period follows)

### ✅ **NATURAL MEDICAL SLOVENIAN**

**Conversational Medical Language:**
- ✅ "Danes vemo" (Today we know)
- ✅ "Zobozdravniki priporočamo" (We dentists recommend)
- ✅ "Naše izkušnje kažejo" (Our experience shows)
- ✅ "V naši ordinaciji" (In our practice)

**Realistic Reassurance:**

- ✅ "Uspešnost je visoka" (Success rate is high)

**Natural Process Descriptions:**

## IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE

### FOR CONTENT CREATORS:

**Detection Checklist:**
1. ✅ Scan for repetitive academic connectors
2. ✅ Check for passive voice overuse
3. ✅ Identify mechanical listing language
4. ✅ Look for translation-style awkwardness
5. ✅ Spot artificial emotional manipulation
6. ✅ Find generic cultural references

**Authenticity Test:**
1. **Read Aloud Test**: Does it sound like natural Slovenian conversation?
2. **Native Speaker Test**: Would a Slovenian recognize this as authentic?
3. **Conversational Test**: Could you say this in informal conversation?
4. **Regional Test**: Do cultural references show genuine local knowledge?

### FOR QUALITY REVIEWERS:

**Red Flags to Flag:**
- Multiple occurrences of phrases from this list
- Overly formal academic language for general audience
- Repetitive sentence structures and connectors
- Generic cultural references without specific knowledge
- Passive voice dominance in action-oriented content
- Mechanical enumeration instead of natural flow

**Authenticity Standards:**
- Content should sound like educated Slovenian professional
- Cultural references should demonstrate genuine local knowledge
- Language should be appropriate for target audience education level
- Emotional tone should feel genuine, not manufactured

This guide serves as a comprehensive filter for creating authentic Slovenian content that resonates with native speakers and avoids the telltale signs of AI generation.
