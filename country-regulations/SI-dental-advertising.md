## Applicable Laws

### ZAvMS — Zakon o avdiovizualnih medijskih storitvah (Audiovisual Media Services Act)
**Source**: pisrs.si/Pis.web/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO6225

**Article 22(2)**: Audiovisual commercial messages for healthcare activities, healthcare services, or healthcare service providers are **PROHIBITED**.

**Critical nuance**: ZAvMS implements the EU AVMSD directive and primarily targets audiovisual media service providers (broadcasters, streaming platforms). The 2018 AVMSD revision extended coverage to Video Sharing Platforms (VSPs), which includes Facebook, YouTube, and Instagram. This means:
- Technically, the VSP (Meta/Facebook) is required not to host/serve healthcare video ads — the prohibition runs against the platform, not the advertiser directly.
- In practice, Slovenian authorities have not pursued individual dental clinic Facebook video ads under ZAvMS.
- **Practical guidance**: Video ads for dental services carry medium legal risk in Slovenia. Educational/testimonial video content is lower risk than overt commercial video ads. All competitors are currently running video ads without apparent enforcement action.

### ZZDej — Zakon o zdravstveni dejavnosti (Healthcare Activity Act)
Advertising of healthcare activities must be limited to "strokovno korektne informacije" (professionally correct information).

**Prohibited**:
- Emphasizing the commercial interest of the provider
- Misleading claims about outcomes or effectiveness
- Exploitation of patient fear, insecurity, or lack of knowledge
- Unsubstantiated statistics or exceptional-result claims

**Allowed**:
- Factual service descriptions
- Objective price information
- Genuine credentials and qualifications
- Factual comparisons (if verifiable)

### ZVPot — Zakon o varstvu potrošnikov (Consumer Protection Act)
Standard consumer protection rules:
- No deceptive or misleading advertising
- No undignified or indecent advertising
- No exploitation of consumer inexperience or lack of knowledge
- Comparative advertising must be factual and not denigrating

## Soft Law / Professional Ethics

### Kodeks medicinske etike (Medical Ethics Code) — Zdravniška zbornica
**Applies to**: Slovenian-registered doctors and dentists (members of Zdravniška zbornica)
**Does NOT apply to**: Foreign healthcare providers advertising in Slovenia (Croatian, Serbian, Austrian clinics) — this explains why foreign chains advertise more aggressively.

Article 63: Advertising limited to professionally correct information. Must not emphasize commercial interest.

History: Advertising was practically banned until 1992, partially eased in 2016 revision.

**Zdravniška zbornica guidance**: Available at zdravniskazbornica.si/zbornica-za-vas/oglasevanje-in-nedovoljeno-oglasevanje

## Specific Creative Rules

### Before/After Photos
- **Allowed** — but must be: genuine patient results, representative of typical outcomes (not exceptional cases)
- Must hold signed, dated patient consent on file
- Must not be retouched or digitally altered
- Cannot present an atypical result as representative
- Currently **zero** dental whitening advertisers in Slovenia use before/after creative — significant gap opportunity

### Testimonials / Patient Reviews
- **Allowed** with patient consent
- Must be genuine — fabricated or paid testimonials violate ZVPot
- Patient cannot be presented in a way that compromises their dignity or privacy

### Price Promotions
- **Allowed** — factual price information is permitted
- Framing matters: "Cena paketa: €279" (factual) is compliant
- Heavy commercial-emphasis hooks like "SUPER POPUST -60% ONLY TODAY!" risk ZZDej commercial-interest prohibition
- Seasonal promotions (June offer, summer pricing) are widely used in the market without enforcement
- Group/couples discounts: allowed as factual price information

### Medical Device Claims (JAZMP)
- Dental whitening gels with hydrogen peroxide >0.1% are regulated medical devices under JAZMP/ZMedPri-1 (June 2025 revision)
- Advertising must not claim effects beyond what JAZMP registration allows
- Claim: "EU-reguliran gel, 6% H₂O₂" is factual and allowed
- Claim: "Guaranteed 5 shades whiter in one session" requires substantiation

## Practical Guidelines for nasmehpg / Slovenian Dental Clients

### Safe copy patterns
- "Profesionalno beljenje zob v naši ordinaciji. Cena paketa: €279."
- "LumiWhite sistem — EU-certiifican gel, prilagojena šablona, vključeno čiščenje in Theodent pasta."
- "Na voljo termini v juniju. Pokliči ali rezerviraj online."
- Patient story: "Ana je izboljšala svojo barvo za 3 odtenke. Preberite njeno izkušnjo." (with consent)

### Copy to avoid
- "UGODNE CENE!" / "NAJCENEJŠE BELJENJE V SLOVENIJI!" — commercial emphasis, superlative claims
- "Zagotovljeni rezultati v 1 obisku!" — unsubstantiated guarantee
- Fear-based hooks like "Ste sramežljivi zaradi rumenih zob?" without immediate constructive framing
- "Popust samo danes!" / countdown urgency — aggressive commercial pressure

### Before/After recommendation
- Before/after images are a significant gap in the Slovenian market — no dental whitening advertiser is currently using them
- Allowed if: genuine patient, signed dated consent on file, results typical/representative
- Use shade guide reference (e.g. "A3 → A1") alongside photo — adds factual basis
- State: "Rezultati se razlikujejo od posameznika do posameznika."

### Video ads
- Educational video (mechanism explanation, patient journey, service walkthrough): Low risk
- Pure 15-second commercial with price hook: Medium risk (ZAvMS grey zone)
- Recommended approach: Start with image/carousel creatives, test educational video, monitor for compliance concerns
