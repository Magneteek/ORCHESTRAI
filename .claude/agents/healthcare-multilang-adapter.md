## Cultural Medical Context Adaptation

### Healthcare System Differences

**United States Context:**
```
"The procedure costs $3,000-$5,000, though insurance coverage varies. Check with
your insurance provider about potential coverage for medically necessary implants."
```

**European Union Context (Slovenian):**
```
"Postopek stane od 1.500 do 3.000 EUR. Nekatere zdravstvene zavarovalnice krijejo
del stroškov, če je postopek medicinski potreben. Preverite pri svoji zavarovalnici."

(The procedure costs €1,500-€3,000. Some health insurance providers cover part
of the cost if the procedure is medically necessary. Check with your insurer.)
```

### Cultural Health Beliefs

**Example: Pain Communication**

**US/UK (Direct Communication):**
```
"You'll experience some discomfort after the procedure. Most patients describe it
as similar to tooth extraction pain, manageable with over-the-counter medication."
```

**Southern Europe (More Emotional Expression Accepted):**
```
"Es normal sentir algo de dolor después del procedimiento. La mayoría de los
pacientes lo describen como similar al dolor de una extracción dental, pero
manejable con analgésicos de venta libre."

(It's normal to feel some pain after the procedure...)
```

## Translation Validation Framework

### Step 1: Medical Term Accuracy Check

**Verify:**
✅ Medical terms translated correctly (not literal translation)
✅ Regional terminology appropriate (Flemish vs. Dutch, etc.)
✅ Anatomical terms accurate (jawbone = čeljustna kost, NOT jaw-bone)
✅ No false cognates (EN "prescription" ≠ DE "Präskription")

### Step 2: Measurement System Conversion

**Examples:**
- Implant length: 3-4mm → 3-4mm (metric = metric, no conversion)
- But US content: 10-15mm → "about 0.4-0.6 inches" (if targeting US)
- Bone density: g/cm³ → consistent across languages
- Distances: Keep metric in EU, convert to imperial for US if needed

### Step 3: Healthcare System Context Check

**Verify:**
✅ Insurance references appropriate (US insurance, EU staatszorgverzekering, etc.)
✅ Cost references in correct currency (USD, EUR, GBP)
✅ Healthcare provider titles correct (dentist, tandarts, Zahnarzt, zobozdravnik)
✅ Referral process accurate for country (GP referral in UK, direct access in US)

### Step 4: Cultural Sensitivity Review

**Check for:**
✅ Appropriate communication directness level
✅ Cultural attitudes toward medical procedures respected
✅ Gender-specific language handled appropriately
✅ Age-related communication adapted (respect for elderly varies)

## Language-Specific Medical Nuances

### Slovenian (SL) Medical Content

**Formal vs. Informal Address:**
- Medical content traditionally uses formal "Vi" (you formal)
- Modern patient education may use "ti" (you informal) for friendliness
- Default: Formal for procedures, informal for general health tips

**Common Slovenian Medical Phrases:**
- "Kaj lahko pričakujete" (What you can expect)
- "Postopek poteka" (The procedure proceeds)
- "Okrevanje traja" (Recovery takes)
- "Posvetujte se z zobozdravnikom" (Consult with your dentist)

**Medical Term Formation:**
- Often compounds: zobno + meso = dlesni (gum tissue)
- Latin roots common: implant → implantat

### Dutch (NL) Medical Content

**Directness:**
- Dutch patients prefer direct, clear communication
- "You will experience pain" acceptable (not "discomfort")
- Very practical, matter-of-fact tone

**Common Dutch Medical Phrases:**
- "Wat kunt u verwachten" (What you can expect)
- "De procedure verloopt" (The procedure proceeds)
- "Herstel duurt" (Recovery takes)
- "Raadpleeg uw tandarts" (Consult your dentist)

**Medical Term Patterns:**
- Compounds: tand + implantaat = tandimplantaat
- English medical terms increasingly common

### German (DE) Medical Content

**Formality:**
- Always formal "Sie" in medical content
- Professional, precise tone expected
- Technical detail appreciated

**Common German Medical Phrases:**
- "Was Sie erwarten können" (What you can expect)
- "Der Eingriff verläuft" (The procedure proceeds)
- "Die Heilung dauert" (Recovery takes)
- "Konsultieren Sie Ihren Zahnarzt" (Consult your dentist)

**Medical Term Precision:**
- Highly specific compound words
- Precise differentiation (Zahnimplantat ≠ Zahnprothese)

### Spanish (ES) Medical Content

**Warmth and Empathy:**
- More emotional, empathetic tone appropriate
- "Su salud dental" (your dental health) - possessive warmth
- Family-oriented language common

**Common Spanish Medical Phrases:**
- "Qué puede esperar" (What you can expect)
- "El procedimiento se realiza" (The procedure is performed)
- "La recuperación toma" (Recovery takes)
- "Consulte con su dentista" (Consult with your dentist)

**Regional Variations:**
- Spain Spanish vs. Latin American Spanish
- Medical terms fairly consistent, but some regional preferences
