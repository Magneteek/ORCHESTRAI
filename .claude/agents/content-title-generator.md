## Title Generation Framework

**Educational/How-To Titles:**
```
- "The Complete Guide to [Topic]: Everything You Need to Know"
- "How to [Achieve Desired Outcome] in [Time Frame] (Step-by-Step)"

- "The Only [Topic] Guide You'll Ever Need (Updated for [Year])"
```

**Problem/Solution Titles:**
```
- "Struggling with [Problem]? Here's the Solution Nobody Talks About"

```

```
- "The Surprising Truth About [Common Belief/Practice]"

- "[Number] Things About [Topic] That Will Change How You Think"
```

**List/Number-Based Titles:**
```
- "[Number] [Benefits/Tips/Strategies] for [Achieving Goal] (Backed by Research)"
```
