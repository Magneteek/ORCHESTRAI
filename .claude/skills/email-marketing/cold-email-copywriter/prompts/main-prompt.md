## Advanced Methodologies

**PREP Framework:**
- **Personalize**: Deep research and relevant personalization
- **Relate**: Connect through shared experiences or challenges
- **Explain**: Clear value proposition with specific benefits
- **Propose**: Specific, low-commitment next step

**BAB (Before-After-Bridge):**
- **Before**: Current problematic state
- **After**: Desired future state
- **Bridge**: Your solution as the path to transformation

**Hook-Story-Close:**
- **Hook**: Attention-grabbing opening with personalization
- **Story**: Relevant case study or insight that demonstrates value
- **Close**: Soft ask for conversation or specific next step

## Sequence Architecture

**5-Touch Cold Sequence:**
1. **Email 1**: Personalized introduction with specific value proposition
2. **Email 2**: Industry insight or case study relevant to their situation  
3. **Email 3**: Social proof from similar company with specific results
4. **Email 4**: Direct value offer (resource, audit, consultation)
5. **Email 5**: Soft break-up with door left open for future

**Timing Strategy:**
- Email 1 → Email 2: 3 business days
- Email 2 → Email 3: 1 week
- Email 3 → Email 4: 2 weeks  
- Email 4 → Email 5: 1 month

## Quality Standards

**Email Quality Checklist:**
- [ ] Specific personalization based on research (not generic)
- [ ] Clear value proposition within first 2 sentences
- [ ] Relevant social proof or credibility indicator
- [ ] Single, clear call-to-action
- [ ] Mobile-optimized formatting (short paragraphs)
- [ ] Professional but conversational tone
- [ ] Spelling and grammar perfection
- [ ] Compliance with anti-spam regulations

**Research Standards:**
- Minimum 3 personalization data points per prospect
- Industry relevance verified for all messaging
- Company-specific insights integrated naturally
- Recent activity or news referenced when available
- Mutual connections leveraged appropriately
