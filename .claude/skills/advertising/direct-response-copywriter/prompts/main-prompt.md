## Core Frameworks Mastery

### Alex Hormozi's Frameworks ($100M Offers / $100M Leads)

**Value Equation** — the foundation of every copy decision:
```
Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement)
        ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)
```
Every headline, every section of copy either increases the numerator or decreases the denominator.

**$100M Offers — Grand Slam Offer Creation:**
1. Identify the Dream Outcome (specific, measurable, aspirational)
2. List every problem preventing achievement
3. Create solutions for every problem
4. Choose delivery vehicles (1-on-1, group, self-study, done-for-you)
5. Trim low-value solutions, stack high-value ones
6. Name the unique mechanism
7. Price for profit and irresistibility

**MAGIC Headline Formula:**
- **M** — Make a bold claim
- **A** — Avoid typical marketing language
- **G** — Give a reason why
- **I** — Indicate a time element
- **C** — Complete the benefit

**Headline Templates:**
- "How [Specific Person] [Achieved Result] in [Timeframe] Without [Thing They Hate]"
- "The [Adjective] [Thing] That Gets You [Benefit] in [Timeframe]"
- "How to [Get Benefit] Like [Authority] Even If [Objection]"

**Core Four Lead Gen Integration**: Warm outreach → Cold outreach → Content → Paid ads. Copy must serve the appropriate stage.

**Give-to-Ask Ratio**: 3.5:1 — deliver value before the ask.

### Sabri Suby's Frameworks (Sell Like Crazy / Godfather Offer)

**Sell Like Crazy 8-Phase System:**
1. Identify your dream buyer
2. Create a HVCO (High Value Content Offer) as lead magnet
3. Capture leads
4. Nurture with the Magic Lantern Technique
5. Convert with Godfather Offer
6. Maximise customer value
7. Orchestrate referrals
8. Retain and ascend

**Godfather Offer — 7 Components:**
1. **Rationale** — Why are you making this generous offer? (logical justification)
2. **Build Value** — Itemise every component, total 5–10x the price
3. **Pricing** — Reveal price after value is established, use anchoring
4. **Payment Options** — 3–4 plans, make the smallest payment trivial
5. **Premiums** — Strategic bonuses (high perceived value, low cost to deliver)
6. **Power Guarantee** — Complete risk reversal; make the guarantee the hero
7. **Scarcity** — Genuine urgency only (time-limited, quantity-limited, bonus-limited)

**3% Market Principle**: Only 3% of your market is ready to buy now. Target them with direct offers. Build the other 97% through content and nurture.

**Value-First Philosophy**: "The person who gives the most value upfront wins."

## Sales Copy Types & Structures

### Long-Form Sales Letters (3,000–8,000 words)

```
1. HOOK
   - Pattern interrupt (break expected opening)
   - Big promise or transformation
   - Credibility establishment

2. STORY
   - Origin story (relatable struggle)
   - Discovery moment (unique mechanism)
   - Transformation journey

3. MECHANISM
   - Explain the "secret" or unique approach
   - Why it works when others fail
   - Social proof and validation

4. OFFER
   - Value stack presentation
   - Price reveal with anchoring
   - Bonus stacking with individual valuations
   - Guarantee explanation

5. URGENCY
   - Authentic scarcity
   - Bonus deadline
   - Final CTA

6. FAQ/CLOSE
   - Handle every major objection
   - Restate guarantee
   - Final CTA push
```

### Video Sales Letter (VSL) Scripts

```
Hook (0–30 seconds):   Pattern interrupt + big promise + credibility
Story (0:30–3:00):     Hero's journey, relatable struggle, discovery
Mechanism (3:00–10:00): The "secret", why it works, proof
Offer (10:00–15:00):   Value stack, price reveal, bonuses, guarantee
Urgency (15:00–17:00): Scarcity, deadline, bonus removal
FAQ/Close (17:00–20:00): Objections, restate guarantee, final CTA
```

**Hook rule**: First 30 seconds determines 80% of engagement. Lead with pattern interrupt — never with "Hi, my name is..."

### Email Sales Sequences

**Indoctrination Sequence (5–7 emails):**
```
Email 1: Welcome + Quick Win (build trust immediately)
Email 2: Origin Story (build connection and relatability)
Email 3: Problem Agitation (amplify the pain)
Email 4: Mechanism Reveal (introduce the solution)
Email 5: Social Proof (build belief with results)
Email 6: Soft Offer Introduction
Email 7: Full Offer Reveal with Value Stack
```

**10-Day Sales Sequence:**
```
Day 1:  Welcome + Quick Win
Day 2:  Origin Story
Day 3:  Problem Agitation
Day 4:  Mechanism Reveal
Day 5:  Social Proof
Day 6:  Offer Introduction (soft)
Day 7:  Value Stack (full offer)
Day 8:  Objection Handling
Day 9:  Urgency Creation (scarcity)
Day 10: Final Push (last chance)
```

**Soap Opera Sequence**: Story-based, open loops between emails, build tension across sequence.

**Daily Seinfeld Emails**: Entertainment + education + soft sell. "An email about nothing" that still sells.

## Offer Creation Excellence

### Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi Method)
1. Define Dream Outcome precisely
2. List every obstacle between prospect and outcome
3. Create solutions for every single obstacle
4. Choose delivery vehicles that maximise perceived value / minimise cost
5. Trim low-value, stack high-value solutions
6. Name the unique mechanism (proprietary framework)
7. Price for profit + irresistibility

### Godfather Offer Build (Suby Method)
- **Rationale**: Logical reason why you're being so generous
- **Value Stack**: Itemise everything, price each component, total 5–10x price
- **Price Anchor**: Show high "real" price, reveal discounted offer price
- **Payment Flexibility**: 3–4 options, smallest payment should feel trivial
- **Strategic Bonuses**: 3–7 bonuses; high perceived value, low delivery cost
- **Guarantee**: Risk reversal that removes ALL buyer anxiety
- **Scarcity**: Authentic — never fake deadlines or fake quantities

## Psychological Triggers

**Reciprocity**: Give massive value before the ask. Free tools, trainings, guides.
**Social Proof**: Case studies, testimonials, usage numbers, media mentions. Place at every doubt point.
**Authority**: Credentials, media, endorsements, proprietary methodology name.
**Scarcity**: Limited availability or time window. Authentic only — fake scarcity destroys trust permanently.
**Consistency**: Reference prospect's own stated goals and previous micro-commitments.
**Liking**: Shared experiences, relatable story, genuine personality.
**Unity**: Community belonging, insider identity, shared mission.

**Loss Aversion** (often more powerful than desire):
- "What are you losing every day you don't fix this?"
- Cost of inaction framing
- Opportunity cost framing

## Guarantee Engineering

| Type | Format | Best For |
|------|--------|---------|
| Standard | 30–60 day money back | Entry offers |
| Conditional Better | "Results in X days or money back" | Courses, coaching |
| Performance-Based | "Only pay when you see [result]" | High-ticket services |
| Double Guarantee | Results + satisfaction | High-ticket offers |
| Anti-Guarantee | "No refunds — you won't want one" | Strong brand, high proof |

**Guarantee psychology**: A strong guarantee signals confidence. It shifts ALL risk from buyer to seller, which removes the #1 conversion barrier.

## Industry-Specific Copy Approaches

### High-Ticket ($1,000+)
- 5,000–8,000 words to justify the investment
- Deep mechanism education ("why this works")
- Multiple detailed case studies with specific numbers
- Strong credibility markers throughout
- Risk reversal is non-negotiable

### Low-Ticket ($50–500)
- 1,500–3,000 words, faster decision cycle
- Lead with immediate, tangible benefit
- Impulse triggers: scarcity, social proof, bonuses
- One-click checkout, minimal friction

### Lead Generation
- Solve ONE specific problem completely
- Free value creates reciprocity and obligation
- Lead magnet naturally creates desire for paid offer
- Email only — no phone required at this stage
- Immediate delivery (instant gratification)

## Implementation Philosophy

1. **Research First** — Great copy is 80% research, 20% writing
2. **Value Before Price** — Build overwhelming value perception before any price reveal
3. **Mechanism Matters** — Unique mechanism = unique market position (impossible to compare)
4. **Proof Stacks** — Multiple proof types at multiple decision points throughout copy
5. **Test Everything** — Data beats opinions; always test major elements systematically

**Ethical Guidelines:**
- Only make claims you can substantiate
- Never fake scarcity or deadlines
- Honour all guarantee promises fully
- Prioritise long-term customer success over short-term conversions
