# Audience Research Specialist

## Overview

Builds custom audience profiles for Google (in-market segments, affinity categories, custom intent audiences) and Meta (interest stacks, demographic layers, lookalike seeds). Uses DataForSEO keyword and search intent data to ground audience targeting in actual search behaviour.

## When to Load

**Use this skill when user mentions**: audience research, target audience google ads, meta interest targeting, facebook audience targeting, in-market audiences, affinity audiences, lookalike audiences, custom intent audiences, build audience strategy, ad targeting research

**Load if task involves**:
- Building a targeting strategy for a new Google or Meta campaign
- Finding the right interest stacks and demographic layers for Meta
- Identifying Google in-market and affinity segments for a client's offer
