# Paid Advertising Pipeline Skill

## Overview

Orchestrates a complete paid campaign launch from brief to launch-ready package. Takes a business, offer, and budget — produces offer architecture, platform-specific copy decks with A/B variations, campaign structure spec, landing page brief, and a UTM-tagged launch checklist. Covers Google Ads, Meta, LinkedIn, and Reddit. Channel selection is prescriptive based on audience, goal, and budget — not exhaustive.
