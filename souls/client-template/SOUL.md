## Content Standards

### Format Preferences

### Evidence Requirements
```
Claims Must Be:
- Quantified: ["30% time savings" not "faster workflows"]
- Verified: [Customer quotes, audit results, clinical studies]

- Compliant: [HIPAA-compliant messaging, no false medical claims]
```

## Output Quality Gates

### Client-Specific Quality Criteria
```
Before Any Deliverable:
□ HIPAA compliance verified (no PHI exposure, compliant claims)
□ Clinical accuracy checked (medical facts peer-reviewed if possible)
□ Accessibility standards met (WCAG AA minimum)
□ Brand voice alignment (matches examples above)
□ Measurement verification (all claims have data sources)
□ Legal review triggers (list what needs legal sign-off)
```

### Blocking Issues
```
Do Not Publish If:
- Medical claims unsupported by evidence
- HIPAA compliance questionable
- Competitor comparisons unverifiable
- ROI claims without customer validation
- Technical depth wrong for audience
```
