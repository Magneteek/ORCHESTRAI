## **ICP Tried These Things But Likely Had These Experiences**

**1. Generic Marketing Automation Platforms (HubSpot, Mailchimp, etc.)**

- **What they tried:** Implemented popular, one-size-fits-all marketing automation platforms
- **Why they tried it:** Attracted by brand recognition, lower cost, and promises of "easy setup"
- **Why it didn't work:** Platforms lacked dental industry-specific understanding, couldn't address technical decision-making processes, and resulted in generic messaging that failed to build trust with dental professionals who have unique compliance and technical concerns

**2. Manual Lead Nurturing with Sales Team**

- **What they tried:** Assigned sales reps to manually follow up with leads through personal emails and phone calls
- **Why they tried it:** Believed personal touch was essential for relationship-driven dental industry
- **Why it didn't work:** Sales reps focused only on hottest opportunities, leaving 79% of leads to never convert; manual processes couldn't scale consistently, and reps spent too much time on administrative tasks instead of closing deals

**3. Basic CRM Implementation Without Automation**

- **What they tried:** Invested in CRM systems like Salesforce but relied on manual data entry and follow-up
- **Why they tried it:** Wanted to organize contacts and track interactions more professionally
- **Why it didn't work:** 60% of professionals admit they're not using their current tools to full capacity; without automation, leads still fell through cracks, response times remained slow, and they couldn't compete with the 35-50% advantage that first responders achieve

**4. In-House Marketing Automation Setup**

- **What they tried:** Attempted to build and manage marketing automation systems with internal team
- **Why they tried it:** Wanted full control and believed their team understood the business best
- **Why it didn't work:** 33% of companies cite lack of internal expertise as the #1 barrier; 35% lack sufficient internal resources; teams got overwhelmed with technical complexity while 24% of leadership failed to properly prioritize the initiative, leading to stalled or abandoned projects

**5. Traditional Marketing Approaches (Trade Shows, Direct Sales)**

- **What they tried:** Continued relying heavily on trade shows, cold calling, and face-to-face relationship building
- **Why they tried it:** Believed dental industry was too relationship-focused for digital approaches
- **Why it didn't work:** Buyer behavior shifted dramatically—65% now prefer remote interactions over face-to-face meetings; 80% of buying process happens via digital research without sales involvement; only 17% of buyer's time is spent meeting with suppliers, making traditional approaches increasingly ineffective
