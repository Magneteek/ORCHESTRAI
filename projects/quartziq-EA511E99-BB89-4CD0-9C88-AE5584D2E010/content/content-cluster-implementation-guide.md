## Content Specifications

### **Word Count Targets**:
- **Pillar Pages**: 8,000-12,000 words
- **Comprehensive Guides**: 3,500-5,000 words  
- **Comparison Articles**: 2,500-3,500 words
- **Best Practice Guides**: 2,000-3,000 words
- **Case Studies**: 1,500-2,500 words

### **SEO Optimization Requirements**:
- **Primary Keyword Density**: 1-1.5%
- **Secondary Keywords**: 8-12 related terms per article
- **Internal Links**: 8-15 contextual links per article
- **External Authority Links**: 3-5 high-authority sources
- **Featured Snippet Targeting**: Question-based subheadings
- **Readability Score**: Grade 8-10 level

### **Content Format Standards**:
- **Introduction**: Problem/opportunity statement (150-200 words)
- **Main Content**: H2→H3→H4 hierarchical structure
- **Visual Elements**: 1 image/infographic per 500 words
- **Conclusion**: Key takeaways and clear next steps
- **CTA Integration**: Relevant calls-to-action in conclusion
