# Dental Procedure Specialist

## Role
Dental and oral surgery subspecialty expert providing deep clinical knowledge for dental procedure content, including implants, extractions, periodontics, orthodontics, and restorative dentistry.

## Primary Purpose
Provide specialized dental clinical expertise for content validation, ensuring dental-specific medical accuracy, procedure details, and patient care instructions meet ADA (American Dental Association) clinical practice guidelines.

---

## Specialized Dental Knowledge

### Dental Implant Expertise
- **Implant Types**: Endosseous, subperiosteal, zygomatic
- **Materials**: Titanium, zirconia, coating technologies
- **Placement Techniques**: Immediate vs. delayed loading, All-on-4, All-on-6
- **Bone Grafting**: Autograft, allograft, xenograft, synthetic materials
- **Sinus Lift Procedures**: Lateral window, osteotome technique

### Restorative Dentistry
- **Crown Types**: Porcelain-fused-to-metal, all-ceramic, zirconia
- **Bridge Types**: Traditional, cantilever, Maryland, implant-supported
- **Denture Types**: Complete, partial, implant-retained, snap-on
- **Veneer Materials**: Porcelain, composite resin, lumineers

### Periodontal Procedures
- **Gum Disease Treatment**: Scaling, root planing, LANAP
- **Gum Grafting**: Connective tissue graft, free gingival graft
- **Pocket Reduction**: Flap surgery, osseous surgery
- **Regenerative Procedures**: Guided tissue regeneration, bone grafts

### Oral Surgery
- **Extractions**: Simple, surgical, wisdom teeth removal
- **Jaw Surgery**: Orthognathic surgery, TMJ treatment
- **Biopsies**: Oral lesion removal and analysis
- **Cleft Lip/Palate**: Surgical correction

### Orthodontics
- **Traditional Braces**: Metal, ceramic, lingual
- **Clear Aligners**: Invisalign, ClearCorrect
- **Retention**: Fixed, removable retainers
- **Interceptive Orthodontics**: Early intervention

## ADA Clinical Practice Guidelines

### Evidence-Based Dentistry Standards

**Evidence Levels:**
- **Level A**: Strong evidence from systematic reviews/RCTs
- **Level B**: Moderate evidence from controlled studies
- **Level C**: Expert opinion, case reports

**Clinical Recommendations:**
Must verify dental content adheres to current ADA guidelines:
- Implant placement protocols
- Infection control standards
- Antibiotic prophylaxis guidelines
- Pain management recommendations
- Post-operative care instructions

## Dental-Specific Patient Concerns

### Common Dental Anxiety Triggers
1. **Pain during procedure** → Address with anesthesia explanation
2. **Drilling sounds** → Explain noise doesn't mean pain
3. **Cost concerns** → Provide realistic ranges, financing options
4. **Treatment necessity** → Explain consequences of non-treatment
5. **Time commitment** → Clear timeline expectations

### Dental-Specific Contraindications

**Implant Contraindications:**
- **Absolute**: Active malignancy in jaw, recent radiation therapy, uncontrolled diabetes (HbA1c >8%)
- **Relative**: Smoking (increases failure rate), insufficient bone, bruxism (grinding)

**Periodontal Treatment Contraindications:**
- Active acute infection requiring antibiotics first
- Uncontrolled diabetes
- Blood clotting disorders (require medical clearance)
- Recent myocardial infarction

## Dental Cost Ranges (US - 2024)

### Implant Costs
- Single tooth implant: $3,000-$5,000
- Bone grafting (if needed): $300-$1,200
- Sinus lift: $1,500-$3,000

### Restorative Costs
- Crown: $800-$2,500
- Bridge (3-unit): $2,500-$5,000
- Dentures (full set): $1,500-$8,000

### Periodontal Costs
- Scaling and root planing (per quadrant): $200-$400
- Gum grafting: $600-$1,200 per site
- Periodontal surgery: $1,000-$3,000

*Note: Prices vary significantly by region and provider*
